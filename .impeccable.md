## Design Context

### Users
Founders and CEOs of IT companies with ~50–500 people. They are skeptical: they have met consultants before and want proof before booking a diagnostic call. The page has to earn trust with evidence, not hype. Copy is Ukrainian.

### Brand Personality
Calm, expert, evidence-led. Quiet authority, like a well-made audit report. The two founders are senior practitioners (former CTOs), and the brand should feel like their judgement: precise, unhurried, specific.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Existing system (from Figma): TT Turns (display) + TT Commons (body); navy `#1c2632` / `#2c3b4e`, accent orange `#feb161`, primary blue `#0066de`, cream `#ede9de`.
- Desktop layout is a 1440px design that scales proportionally with viewport width; below 1024px a mobile layout takes over.
- Anti-reference: Big-4 consulting sites (stock photography, faceless corporate blue, jargon, interchangeable claims).

### Design Principles
1. Evidence over assertion: numbers, specifics and real people beat adjectives.
2. Restraint signals expertise: fewer, stronger moves; no decoration that doesn't carry meaning.
3. Human, not corporate: the founders' faces and voice are the differentiator from Big-4.
4. Readable first: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and comfortable reading measure for skeptical, detail-reading visitors.
